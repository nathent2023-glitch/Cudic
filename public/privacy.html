Privacy policy

How Cudic handles your data: accounts, projects, cookies, and advertising. Plain version: we store what you make and what you need to sign in, advertisers store ad cookies, and we never sell your personal information.

Last updated 11 October 2026.

Account data

When you create an account (email + password, GitHub, or guest session), we store your email address, display name, profile details you add (bio, interests, avatar), and a user id. Authentication and the database are run by our backend provider (Supabase). We use this data to sign you in, show your profile to other users, and keep your projects, game saves, coins, and liked games attached to your account.

Things you publish

Games, theme packs, servers, chat messages, comments, and project files you save or publish are stored so the site works — other people can play your games, read your messages, and remix your work. Anything you publish is visible to other users by design. Don't publish anything you want to keep private.

Local storage and site cookies

Cudic keeps small pieces of data in your browser (local storage and cookies): your sign-in session, theme and accent preferences, editor settings, and a session seat id that keeps one login active per account. This data stays on your device except where the site needs it to function, and clearing your browser data signs you out.

Advertising

Cudic shows ads to keep the site free. Our ad partner Google uses cookies to serve ads based on your visits to this and other sites — including the DoubleClick cookie, web beacons, and IP addresses collected as part of ad serving. Google may use this data for personalized advertising.

You can opt out of personalized advertising at Google Ads Settings and aboutads.info. You can also block or delete cookies in your browser settings, though signing in and some features may stop working. Read how Google uses data on How Google uses information from sites that use its services.

Third-party services

  • Supabase — authentication, database, and file storage for accounts and projects.
  • Google AdSense — advertising, as described above.
  • Content delivery networks (e.g. jsDelivr, Google Fonts) — libraries and fonts the pages load. These providers may log requests as part of normal operation.

Children

Cudic is a general-audience site also used by students. It is not directed at children under 13, and under-13s should only use it with a parent or guardian's permission and supervision. If you believe a child has given us personal information without consent, tell us and we will delete it.

Your choices

You can edit your display name, bio, and avatar on your profile page at any time, unpublish or delete your games from Studio, and leave servers you joined. To delete your account and personal data entirely, contact the site operator through the chat or your app store listing and we will remove it.

Changes to this policy

If this policy changes in a meaningful way, the updated date above will change and continued use of the site counts as acceptance. For questions about this policy, reach out through the site chat.
