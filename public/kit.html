Cudic Kit

One stylesheet and one small script. Flat colour, 3px ink borders, hard offset shadows, and a press you can feel. Put class="ck" on a container — everything inside is styled, nothing leaks out.

cudic-kit.css · cudic-kit.js · csel.js for dropdowns

Colour

Warm paper, ink, and four accents. Every accent fill carries ink text — that is the one rule that keeps the look and keeps contrast above 4.5:1.

Dark mode flips paper and ink; the accents stay. :root[data-theme="dark"] .ck

Buttons

Hover lifts the button off the paper, press slams it flat. ck-btn + ck-btn-primary, ck-btn-sm, ck-btn-ghost.

Fields

Inputs sit in a raised well and press in on focus. Labels, hints and errors are separate so a form can be honest about what went wrong.

Dropdowns

The native <select> stays the source of truth; csel.js draws the button and menu. Changing the menu fires a real change event, so no page logic has to know.

Check, radio, switch, range

Real inputs with the boxes drawn on top — keyboard and screen readers keep working. The switch knob overshoots and settles, which is most of its charm.

Chips

Filter and label chips. aria-pressed="true" fills them primary — the toggle is the state, not a separate class.

Tabs

CudicKit.tabs(el) wires the buttons to the panels and keeps aria-selected honest. Arrow keys move between tabs.

Cards

ck-card is the base surface; ck-card-hover lifts a whole card on hover (use it when the card is a link).

Dialog, confirm, toast

<dialog class="ck-dialog"> gets the pop-in and the dimmed backdrop for free. CudicKit.confirm() wraps it in a promise. Toasts stack bottom-centre and never block a click.

Empty, loading, skeletons

Every empty state is the same shape: a small plate, what happened, one line, one action.
